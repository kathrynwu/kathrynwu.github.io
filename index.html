Kathryn Wu

Building the agentic AI search engine for SMB data.

I'm the co-founder of Openmart (YC W24), where we're building an agentic search and enrichment engine for the long tail of small and medium-sized businesses. Our technology helps teams find, understand, and reach businesses that traditional data providers often miss.

Openmart is Clay’s primary SMB data vendor and has supported teams at Whatnot and DoorDash.

About

I studied computer science at UC Berkeley and started my career in engineering. Over time, I realized what energizes me most is the intersection of people, product, and growth: understanding customers, turning their needs into products, and building the go-to-market strategies that make businesses grow.

I work across marketing, product, and revenue, and have built an audience of 50K+ followers across platforms. I love figuring out what customers care about and turning that empathy into growth.

I also bootstrapped an e-commerce brand, which gave me firsthand experience with the challenges small businesses face.
